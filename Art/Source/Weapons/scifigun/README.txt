GL-11 GUNLANCE — COMPLETE FREE WEAPON / 1.0

QUICK START
1. Easiest portable import: model/gunlance-animated.glb. Play Gunlance_Deploy.
2. Easiest Blender playback: model/gunlance-baked.blend. Press Space on the timeline.
3. Edit the original controls and LODs: model/gunlance-controls.blend. Select GUNLANCE_CONTROLS; Deploy and Energy are 0..1, Length_m defaults 11. These properties are keyed; remove their keys before manual adjustment. Enable only one R2_LOD collection at a time.
4. Timeline: frame 1 closed, 48 open without energy,72 energy extended,100 holds. 30 fps. The 3.3s deployment does not loop smoothly. No firing effect or combat animation is supplied.
5. model/gunlance-closed-static.fbx is a CLOSED STATIC fallback. It has no animation. Reconnect included texture maps if your importer does not wire them automatically.

CONTENTS AND COUNTS
One complete weapon in 4 model files / 3 formats, not four unique weapons.
LOD0: 22,128 triangles,10 mesh objects, 46,856 GLB vertex entries (normal/UV/tangent seams duplicate vertices).
LOD1: 6,976 triangles; LOD2: 1,994 triangles. Lower LODs are in the controls BLEND only.
3 source PNG maps at 2048x2048: BaseColor (sRGB), Normal (OpenGL+Y, non-color), ORM (R=occlusion,G=roughness,B=metalness, non-color). BaseColor alpha encodes the inverse polymer-window mask.
GLB has 3 materials and embedded textures. Original controls BLEND has 2 materials with custom Blender shading.
Authored closed length 11 meters. This is a large sci-fi weapon; scale for your scene. No automatic hand fitting or mount system.

MATERIAL / ANIMATION LIMITS
The controls BLEND retains the original procedural energy/glass shader and simple arithmetic controls. The baked BLEND and GLB use portable PBR, alpha transparency and constant cyan emission. Energy and polymer appearance differ between renderers; no bloom or screen effect is supplied. FBX material wiring may need adjustment. The animation moves rigid mesh parts and has no skeletal character rig.
The gallery shows the actual delivered GLB appearance. No character, ship, gameplay controller, collision setup or engine prefab is included. Engine performance is untested.
